Echo Grid

Privacy Policy

Echo Grid is a local-first arcade game with no account or network requirement.

Last updated: August 3, 2026
On this pageLocal dataDiagnosticsChoicesContact

1. Local game data

Runs, best scores, settings, language, and progression remain on the device. Echo Grid does not require an account and does not upload gameplay, contacts, photos, messages, microphone audio, or precise location. The game has no in-game advertising in its current design.

2. Crash diagnostics

When enabled in a release build, Crashlytics may receive limited technical diagnostics needed to identify crashes and improve stability. Diagnostics exclude gameplay records, save files, and user-entered content. No advertising profile is created by Kortex.

3. Your choices and deletion

You can play offline and clear local progress using the app or device settings. Clearing app data removes the local records from that device.

4. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: support@usekortex.com.
