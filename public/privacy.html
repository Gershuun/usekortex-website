Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

Effective Date: July 11, 2026

Welcome to Kortex! This Privacy Policy applies to our entire suite of applications, including Kortex Contacts, Kortex Captions, and Kortex Filters. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have control over your data.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the Kortex app you use, we may collect the following data locally on your device or via secure OAuth connections:

  • Photos & Media: To process and generate captions/filters (Kortex Captions & Filters).
  • Contact Lists: To optimize and organize your network (Kortex Contacts).
  • Social Profile Data: Name, username, and public posts when you explicitly link accounts like Instagram or LinkedIn.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used strictly for core app functionality (such as analyzing a photo using AI to generate a caption). We do not sell your data, nor do we run background tracking for advertising purposes. We only use official OAuth handshakes to authenticate with third-party networks.

3. Data Deletion Request (Required by Google Play)

We respect your right to be forgotten. If you wish to delete your account, unlink your social media, or request the permanent deletion of any data associated with Kortex, please send an email to:

privacy@usekortex.com

Instructions: Include the subject line "Data Deletion Request" along with the email address or username associated with your account. We will process your request and permanently wipe your data within 7 business days.

4. Data Security

All data transmitted between Kortex apps and our third-party AI or social providers (like Google Gemini and Meta) is encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS protocols.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Please check back for updates.
