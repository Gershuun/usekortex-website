Privacy Policy

Shiftpocalypse keeps runs, career tokens, and settings local to the device.

Last updated: August 3, 2026
On this pageLocal dataServicesDiagnosticsChoicesContact

1. Local game data

Runs, career tokens, settings, language, haptics, and progress remain in local app storage. The game does not require an account and does not collect contacts, photos, messages, microphone audio, or precise location.

2. Optional providers

Rewarded recovery, purchase, or consent features may be enabled by a release. Those providers may process device, advertising, or purchase information under their own policies. Core play remains local and the app does not send workplace characters or event-card choices to Kortex.

3. Crash diagnostics

Crashlytics may receive limited technical diagnostics such as app version, build, route, feature, and generic error context. It does not receive runs, save files, or user-entered content.

4. Your choices and deletion

You can decline optional ads or purchases, reset local progress, or clear app data in device settings. Clearing app data removes local records from that device.

5. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: support@usekortex.com.
