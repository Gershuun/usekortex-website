Privacy Policy

Kortex Routes is a local rehearsal tool for practicing approved routes.

Last updated: August 3, 2026
On this pageRoute dataMaps and filesPurchases and adsDiagnosticsContact

1. Route data

No account is required. Stops, notes, rehearsal progress, settings, and route cards are stored in local SQLite storage. The app rejects student names and sensitive-person columns. Kortex does not send imported route content to its servers.

2. Maps and file access

Document access is requested only when you choose CSV import. When an external map or waypoint provider is used, it may receive the visible map area or lookup requested by the feature under that provider's own policy. The app is not commercial turn-by-turn navigation.

3. Optional purchases and advertising

If Pro access is enabled, RevenueCat and the relevant app store may process purchase and restore information. Google Mobile Ads may process device or advertising information on supported surfaces. Neither provider receives imported student data from Kortex.

4. Crash diagnostics

Crashlytics may receive limited technical diagnostics such as app version, build, route, feature, and generic error context. It does not receive route cards, addresses, or user-entered notes.

5. Your choices and contact

You can avoid CSV import and optional map, ad, or purchase features, export or delete local routes, or clear app data. Questions or privacy requests: support@usekortex.com.
