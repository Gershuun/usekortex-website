Privacy Policy

Kortex Haul calculates pay and offers locally from the assumptions you enter.

Last updated: August 3, 2026
On this pageEntered dataPurchases and adsDiagnosticsChoicesContact

1. Calculator data

Pay assumptions, offers, comparisons, settings, and saved calculations are stored locally on the device. Haul does not require credentials and does not upload your calculator records. Optional extraction features operate only when you choose them and should not be used with private documents unless you understand the service involved.

2. Optional purchases and advertising

If Pro access is enabled, RevenueCat and the relevant app store may process purchase, subscription, and restore information needed to grant the entitlement. Google Mobile Ads may process device or advertising information on supported free surfaces under its own policy.

3. Crash diagnostics

Crashlytics may receive limited technical diagnostics such as app version, build, route, feature, and generic error context. It does not receive pay details, employer names, routes, or user-entered calculation text.

4. Your choices and deletion

You can keep the app offline, avoid optional extraction, decline ads or purchases, export your records, or delete local data from the app or device settings. Results are estimates, not tax, accounting, legal, or employment advice.

5. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: support@usekortex.com.
