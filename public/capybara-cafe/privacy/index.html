Capybara Café

Privacy Policy

Capybara Café keeps your cozy café progress on your device.

Last updated: August 3, 2026
On this pageLocal dataAdvertisingServicesChoicesContact

1. Local game data

No account is required. Café progress, inventory, recipes, friendships, customization, settings, language, and achievements are stored in the app's local save file. Capybara Café does not request contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or precise location for the game.

2. Optional sponsor rewards

The game has no ads during active play. An optional rewarded sponsor may appear at the completed-day summary. Test builds use test ad identifiers; a production release may use approved Google Mobile Ads identifiers. The ad provider may process device or advertising information under its own policy.

3. Diagnostics and platform features

Crashlytics may receive limited technical diagnostics to identify crashes, excluding save files, names, dialogue, and other gameplay content. Optional platform achievements or leaderboards operate only when a supported platform account is configured and are governed by that platform's privacy terms.

4. Your choices and deletion

You can decline optional ads or platform features and clear local progress using the app or device settings. Clearing app data removes the local save from that device.

5. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: support@usekortex.com.
