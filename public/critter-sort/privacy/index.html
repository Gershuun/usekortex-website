Critter Sort

Privacy Policy

Critter Sort keeps puzzles, progress, and collections local to your device.

Last updated: August 3, 2026
On this pageLocal dataAdvertisingServicesChoicesContact

1. Local puzzle data

Critter Sort does not require sign-in or cloud sync. Campaign progress, daily puzzles, scores, coins, themes, accessories, settings, language, and saved sessions stay in local app storage. The game does not collect contacts, photos, messages, microphone audio, or precise location.

2. Ads and optional rescue

Consent-aware Google Mobile Ads may provide a banner on supported surfaces and an optional rewarded fourth rescue cell. Closed-testing builds use test identifiers. The advertising provider may process device or advertising information under its own policy; puzzle state is not sent to Kortex for ad profiling.

3. Diagnostics

Crashlytics may receive limited technical diagnostics such as app version, build, route, feature, and generic error context. It does not receive critter boards, save files, names, or user-entered content.

4. Your choices and deletion

You can decline optional ad rewards, revoke permissions, reset progress in the app, or clear app data in device settings. Clearing app data removes local progress and collections from that device.

5. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: support@usekortex.com.
