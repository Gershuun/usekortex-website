Orbit Snap

Privacy Policy

Orbit Snap is a local-first arcade game with offline play and optional rewards.

Last updated: August 3, 2026
On this pageLocal dataAdvertisingDiagnosticsChoicesContact

1. Local game data

Runs, scores, levels, daily challenges, settings, language, and profile preferences remain on the device. Orbit Snap does not require an account or upload gameplay, contacts, photos, messages, microphone audio, or precise location.

2. Optional rewarded advertising

An optional rewarded ad may provide a recovery or bonus feature. Test builds use test identifiers; a production release may use approved Google Mobile Ads identifiers. The provider may process device or advertising information under its own policy. Ads are not required for ordinary play.

3. Crash diagnostics

Crashlytics may receive limited technical diagnostics such as app version, build, route, feature, and generic error context. It does not receive saved runs, profile data, or user-entered content.

4. Your choices and deletion

You can decline the optional reward, play offline, reset progress, or clear app data in device settings. Clearing app data removes local progress from that device.

5. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: support@usekortex.com.
