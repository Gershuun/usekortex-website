Ten Dash

Privacy Policy

Ten Dash is a local-first arcade game with no account or network requirement.

Last updated: August 3, 2026
On this pageLocal dataDiagnosticsChoicesContact

1. Local game data

Runs, best scores, settings, language, and progression remain on the device. Ten Dash does not require an account and does not upload gameplay, contacts, photos, messages, microphone audio, or precise location. The current game design has no advertising, stamina system, loot box, or paid retry.

2. Crash diagnostics

When enabled in a release build, Crashlytics may receive limited technical diagnostics needed to identify crashes and improve stability. It does not receive gameplay records, save files, or user-entered content.

3. Your choices and deletion

You can play offline and clear local progress using the app or device settings. Clearing app data removes local records from that device.

4. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: support@usekortex.com.
