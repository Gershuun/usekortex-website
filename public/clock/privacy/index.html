Privacy Policy

Kortex Clock keeps workplace planning and attendance records in local storage.

Last updated: August 3, 2026
On this pageRecordsPurchases and adsDiagnosticsChoicesContact

1. Workplace records

Schedules, policies, attendance events, evidence references, settings, and language choices remain in local SQLite storage. The MVP does not use background location or employer credentials and does not require an account or cloud backup.

2. Optional purchases and advertising

If a release enables Pro access, RevenueCat and the relevant app store may process purchase, subscription, and restore information needed to grant the entitlement. Google Mobile Ads may process device or advertising information on supported free surfaces under its own policy.

3. Crash diagnostics

Crashlytics may receive limited technical diagnostics such as app version, build, route, feature, and generic error context. It does not receive workplace records, employee names, schedules, or evidence references.

4. Your choices and deletion

You control what you enter, whether to use purchase or ad features, and whether to export or delete local records. Clearing app data removes the local database; store purchase history remains with the store or RevenueCat according to their policies.

5. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: support@usekortex.com.
