Privacy Policy

Kortex Words keeps puzzles, progress, achievements, and settings on your device.

Last updated: August 3, 2026
On this pageLocal dataAdvertisingPlatform servicesDiagnosticsContact

1. Local puzzle data

Word puzzles, scores, themes, achievements, streaks, settings, and language choices remain in local storage. Words does not require an account or cloud sync and does not upload puzzle state, contacts, photos, messages, microphone audio, or precise location.

2. Advertising

Google Mobile Ads may be enabled on supported screens or optional rewarded features. Test builds use test identifiers and production readiness is validated separately. The provider may process device or advertising information under its own policy.

3. Optional platform services

Future or configured platform achievement and leaderboard services may process the account and score information required by their own policies. Platform services are optional and local play remains available.

4. Crash diagnostics

Crashlytics may receive limited technical diagnostics for stability work. It does not receive word lists, puzzle answers, save files, or user-entered content.

5. Contact and deletion

Reset progress or clear app data to remove local records. Questions or privacy requests: support@usekortex.com.
