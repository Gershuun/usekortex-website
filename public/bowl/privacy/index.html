Privacy Policy

Kortex Bowl is playable offline and keeps game progress on your device.

Last updated: August 3, 2026
On this pageLocal dataPlatform servicesAdvertisingChoicesContact

1. Local game data

Scores, progression, coins, settings, language, and pending platform operations are stored locally. Kortex Bowl does not require an account and does not send raw gameplay, contacts, photos, messages, microphone audio, or precise location to Kortex.

2. Optional platform features

When configured and requested by you, Google Play Games or another platform may process account, achievement, or leaderboard information under that platform's privacy policy. Platform sign-in and score submission are optional and the local save remains authoritative.

3. Advertising and diagnostics

Google Mobile Ads may process device or advertising information when an approved ad placement is enabled. Test builds use test identifiers. Crashlytics may receive limited technical diagnostics, excluding save files and user-entered content.

4. Your choices and deletion

You can decline platform sign-in or optional ads, disconnect platform features, reset local progress, or clear app data in device settings. Provider-held data is controlled through the relevant platform account.

5. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: support@usekortex.com.
