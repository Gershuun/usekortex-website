Kortex Contacts Support

Support for Kortex Contacts mobile app, Pro access, contact cleaning, backups, and CRM connections.

Contact support: support@usekortex.com

What to include

Include your platform and OS version, app version, issue description, and screenshots if available. Do not send contact lists, passwords, API tokens, payment card numbers, or other sensitive data.

Purchases and refunds

For App Store, Google Play, and Amazon purchases, refund requests are handled through the store where the purchase was made. For web purchases, include the purchase email or a redacted license key.

Privacy and data requests

Read our Privacy Policy or email support@usekortex.com for privacy questions and data requests.
