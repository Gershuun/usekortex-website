Route Rush

Privacy Policy

Route Rush is an offline bus-route puzzle with local progress and optional hints.

Last updated: August 3, 2026
On this pageLocal dataAnalyticsAds and purchasesDiagnosticsContact

1. Local game data

Levels, stars, scores, settings, language, and save progress remain on the device. Route Rush does not collect student data and does not request contacts, photos, microphone audio, or precise location for core play.

2. Analytics choices

The development analytics adapter records nothing without consent and rejects route, location, name, text, and content properties. No route or student information is used to build an analytics profile.

3. Optional ads and purchases

Optional capped rewarded hints or a future purchase flow may be enabled by a release. Providers may process device, advertising, or purchase information under their own policies. Test builds use test services where configured.

4. Crash diagnostics

Crashlytics may receive limited technical diagnostics for stability work. It does not receive route boards, save files, student data, or user-entered content.

5. Contact and deletion

You can decline optional analytics, ads, or purchases and clear local progress from the app or device settings. Questions or privacy requests: support@usekortex.com.
