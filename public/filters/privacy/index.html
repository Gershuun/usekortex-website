Privacy Policy for Kortex Filters

Last updated: July 11, 2026

Welcome to Kortex Filters. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use our photo editing application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the photos you explicitly choose to upload to the app for editing purposes. We do not access your photo gallery without your explicit permission.

2. How We Use Your Information

The photos you select are securely transmitted to our backend services (powered by Google Cloud and the Gemini API) solely for the purpose of applying the requested AI filters and edits. We do not use your photos to train our models or for any other purpose.

3. Data Storage and Retention

Photos processed by Kortex Filters are temporarily processed in memory on our servers and are returned to your device. We do not permanently store your photos on our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements within the app. AdMob may collect and use certain data for advertising purposes. We also use Google Cloud Platform for backend processing.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@usekortex.com.
